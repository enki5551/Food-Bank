International movement must follow local food-safety, customs, import, export, animal-feed, and waste-management laws. Fresh cooked food should normally remain local; approved shelf-stable products may use cross-border routes.

Please be human: protect dignity, protect freshness, protect every living being.

Food that cannot safely reach people is never served. Where legally appropriate, it may be diverted to approved animal-feed or composting partners.
